💡 What is Networking?

Networking is the foundation of IT systems that connects devices to exchange data and resources over local and global networks.

  • OSI Model (7 Layers)
  • IP Addressing (IPv4 / IPv6)
  • Subnetting & CIDR
  • DNS & DHCP
  • Routing & Switching

🔌 Network Devices

  • Router: connects different networks
  • Switch: connects devices in LAN
  • Firewall: protects networks
